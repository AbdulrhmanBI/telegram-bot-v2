Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when users interact with the Telegram bot or use its Mini App.

The policy applies to the bot and its Mini App. It describes the information required to operate their features, maintain access controls, deliver requested content, and protect the service.

Bot and Mini App Privacy Policy

1. Who Operates the Service

1.1. The bot and Mini App are an independent project managed by the bot owner.

2. Information Collected

2.1. The bot may process your Telegram ID, username, first name, and last name. These details identify your account, connect your requests to the correct chat, and help the bot owner provide support.

2.2. Button presses, callback actions, menu selections, and navigation within the bot chat are processed to carry out the action you choose and keep the bot's menus and conversation working correctly.

2.3. Membership and access information may be checked to confirm whether your account belongs to a required group and whether access has been restricted. This protects content that is available only to eligible users.

2.4. The Mini App automatically processes requests to load the library and the file references selected for delivery. This is required to display the available content and send the files you request to the correct Telegram account. The bot owner cannot view your browsing, searches, or file selections inside the Mini App.

2.5. The Mini App may store cached library content, your selected-file cart, theme settings, and interface preferences on your device. This allows the app to load efficiently and preserve your selections while you use it.

3. Information Available to the Bot Owner

3.1. Through the bot's administration features, the bot owner may access Telegram profile details, messages and commands sent to the bot, bot-chat interactions, membership and access status, and saved navigation information.

3.2. This access is used to manage the bot, assist users, enforce access requirements, and resolve operational issues.

3.3. The bot owner cannot view users' in-app browsing history, search queries, or selected-file lists. These actions are processed automatically by the Mini App to provide its library and file-delivery features.

4. How Information Is Used

4.1. Information is used only where needed to:

(a) identify the Telegram account making a request and deliver the response to the correct chat;

(b) operate bot commands, menus, search, the content library, and file delivery;

(c) verify required group or channel membership and apply access restrictions;

(d) maintain the bot's navigation state so users can continue from their previous location;

(e) process Mini App file selections and send the requested files to the user's private Telegram chat;

(f) limit excessive requests and reduce spam, misuse, and duplicate actions; and

(g) diagnose errors, maintain performance, and protect the bot and its users.

5. Mini App and File Delivery

5.1. The Mini App verifies Telegram's signed initialization data before providing access. It also applies the bot's block and membership checks to prevent unauthorized access.

5.2. When you select files and press Send, the Mini App sends the selected file references to the bot's system. The system checks that the selected items belong to the published library and then sends the corresponding files to your private chat.

5.3. Telegram file identifiers remain on the server. The Mini App receives short file keys instead, reducing the amount of internal file information exposed to the browser.

5.4. Each send can include up to 10 audio or voice recordings and up to 10 other files, subject to validation and request limits. These limits help keep delivery reliable and prevent excessive requests.

5.5. The selected-file cart and cached library content may remain in your device or browser storage.

6. Third-Party Services and External Content

6.1. The bot uses external infrastructure and services to provide its features:

6.2. (a) Telegram processes bot conversations, provides account information, supports group or channel membership checks, and delivers messages and files;

6.3. (b) Cloudflare hosts the bot's Worker and Mini App and provides the database, key-value storage, and caching used to operate the service; and

7. Storage and Retention

7.1. Telegram profile and access information is stored in the bot's database to recognize users and manage access. Bot navigation paths are stored so users can return to their previous location in the menus.

7.2. The bot owner is not provided with a history of Mini App browsing, search queries, or selected-file choices. The Mini App may keep its cached content and cart locally on the user's device.

7.3. Information is retained for as long as needed to operate the bot, manage access, provide support, prevent misuse, and resolve technical issues. Temporary cache entries expire according to their configured periods.

7.4. The project does not set one fixed deletion period for all user profile and navigation information. Backups and technical logs may follow separate retention cycles.

8. Sharing and Disclosure

8.1. Personal information is not sold or used to build advertising profiles.

8.2. Information may be processed or shared where necessary:

8.3. (a) with Telegram to process bot interactions, verify membership, and deliver requested content;

8.4. (b) with infrastructure providers that host and support the bot and Mini App;

8.5. (c) with the bot owner through administration features, for support, content management, access control, and security; or

8.6. (d) where disclosure is required by law or is necessary to protect users, the bot, or its security.

8.7. Public repository files and information submitted to external websites are subject to the access and privacy practices of those platforms.

9. Security

9.1. The bot uses safeguards that include a secret-protected Telegram webhook, verification of Telegram Mini App initialization data, administrator access checks, and limits on requests and file sending.

9.2. These measures help prevent unauthorized requests, protect account access, and reduce misuse. However, no online service or storage system can guarantee complete security.

9.3. Users should not send passwords or highly sensitive personal information to the bot.

10. Children

10.1. The bot is not intended to collect children's personal information in a way that violates applicable law.

10.2. A parent or guardian who believes a child has shared information inappropriately may contact the bot owner.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

11.1. This policy may be updated when the bot's features, data practices, providers, or applicable requirements change.

11.2. The latest version will be published at the same URL, with the update date changed accordingly. Users should review this page periodically.

12. Contact

12.1. For privacy questions or requests, contact the bot owner.
